## Philosophy
<hr style="border: 0; border-top: 1px solid #d9d9d9; margin-top: 0.3rem; margin-bottom: 1.2rem;">

My teaching is built around a simple principle: students should understand the economic substance behind accounting numbers before learning the mechanics used to produce them. 

I use intuitive business problems and real-world financial statements to help students understand why accounting rules exist before introducing their technical application.

I also incorporate emerging technologies into the classroom by emphasizing how students should evaluate, verify, and take responsibility for AI-assisted accounting analysis rather than simply using technology to produce answers.
